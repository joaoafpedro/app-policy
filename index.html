Personal app: privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

This is a private, single-user application. It is not offered to the public, and only its owner can sign in to it.

Google data

With the owner's consent, the app requests read-only access to the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar (gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly). It uses this access only to show the owner summaries of their own email and schedule. It cannot send, delete or modify email or calendar events.

How data is handled

  • Data is used only to provide the app's features to its owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train general-purpose models.
  • Access credentials are stored only on the owner's device. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
